I shouldn't even be able to ride down this trail at this time of year. There should be a massive snowdrift, or at best the road should be best with muddy conditions and a light cover of snow.

But here we are in the driest, warmest Colorado winter to date. I'm thankful for the opportunity to ride this early in the season, but concerned about what this is going to mean for the summer.
