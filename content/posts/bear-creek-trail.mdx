I shouldn't even be able to ride down this trail at this time of year. There should be a snowdrift, or it should be too slushy to cruise around here without a fat tire setup. At the bare minimum it should at least be too fucking cold for me to be out here.

But alas, here we are in the driest, warmest Colorado winter on record. Thanks, Exxon bros. On the one hand, I'm thankful for the opportunity to cycle this early in the season, but I know what this is going to mean for the summer-- water restrictions, and smoky skies. And every local meteorologist will be bitching about the conditions without so much as a mention of the true root cause-- climate change. And you'll know why they're so tight-lipped, once you see how many ad spots the Colorado Oil and Gas Industry have bought on their networks. Sigh, guess I should just make lemonade out of these lemons. Hell, maybe I'll be able to grow a fucking lemon TREE here pretty soon.

### A Note on the Video

I'm using the entry model Go Pro Hero compact camera, and I've moved on from the chest mount to the handle bar mount which has definitely been an improvement. As for the music, this is just what I've been able to find on the public domain. I am surprised there's so many people that spend the time to make music just put it online and give it away for free without any need for attribution.
