## What This Is

I've been trying to explain this project to people and failing. *It's a blog, sort of. And a video series. And maybe a podcast eventually.* The honest answer is: I don't know yet. I know what it isn't — it isn't a brand, it isn't a personal development platform, it isn't content.

It's closer to a journal. The kind you keep because you're trying to figure something out, not because you want an audience.

## Why Now

Last year I spent a lot of time doing things I didn't believe in for reasons I couldn't defend. The accumulation of that has a weight. This is, in part, the answer to that weight.

*You don't need permission to make something.* This is something I knew but stopped acting on. So — here. Something made.

## What's Coming

More of this. Conversations with people who interest me. Posts about places and food and the strange mechanics of ordinary days. No editorial calendar. No content strategy. No version of this designed to scale.
